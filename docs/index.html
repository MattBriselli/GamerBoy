GamerBoy Solitaire

A crisp, Modern Classic Klondike Solitaire experience crafted for iPhone, iPad, and Android.

Highlights

  • Verified Winning Deals: Play guaranteed-solvable shuffles anytime in Draw 1 or Draw 3 mode.
  • Daily Challenges: Compete on the same seeded board every day and collect monthly crowns.
  • Effortless Controls: Play with one-tap smart moves or smooth multi-card stack drag-and-drop.
  • Unlimited Undo & Auto-Complete: Never lose progress to a mis-tap, with automatic mid-game saves.
  • Three Table Themes: Switch seamlessly between Emerald Felt, Midnight Slate, and Warm Ivory.
