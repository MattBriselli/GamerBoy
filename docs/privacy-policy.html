Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • App: GamerBoy Solitaire

GamerBoy Studio ("we", "our", or "us") built the GamerBoy Solitaire mobile application as an ad-supported, free-to-play game for iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your privacy choices.

1. Game Data Stored Locally on Your Device

GamerBoy Solitaire does not require user accounts, registration, or sign-in. Your in-progress card games, high scores, win streaks, Daily Challenge crowns, and theme preferences are stored strictly locally on your device using on-device storage and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep GamerBoy Solitaire free to play, we display non-intrusive banner advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Depending on your device permissions and regional consent settings, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers (such as Apple IDFA when authorized via App Tracking Transparency, or Google Advertising ID on Android)
  • Approximate Location (inferred from IP address, such as country/city, to serve regionally appropriate ads and comply with local regulations)
  • Ad Interaction & Performance Data (ad views, taps, and basic SDK diagnostics/crash logs to prevent fraud and ensure reliability)

Learn more about how Google processes data from apps that use its services at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your Choices & Tracking Controls

  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS/iPadOS, GamerBoy Solitaire requests your permission before allowing third-party ad networks to access your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are requested. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android Advertising ID Controls: On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID anytime in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • EEA / UK / Regional Consent: Where required by law (including GDPR), a consent prompt is presented via Google's User Messaging Platform prior to loading personalized advertisements.

4. Children's Privacy

GamerBoy Solitaire does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and configures ad requests for general/family-safe content ratings.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised Effective Date.
